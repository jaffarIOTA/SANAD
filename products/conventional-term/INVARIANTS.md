# Conventional term loan invariants

- **C-1** The rate on the offer is a sourced, effective-dated snapshot; no anonymous rate.
- **C-2** The schedule is integer arithmetic; instalments sum exactly to what is owed.
- **C-3** Affordability is a tenant rule with a citation; a quote over the cap is refused.
- **C-4** No approval without the bureau; disbursement once, reported once.
- **C-5** The APR is the platform's, computed from this module's own cash flows.
